# Task: faq-golden (FAQs, synonyms, golden test set) — OpenCode free model

You are a writer. You cannot run commands here: create files only. Read `web/docs/BUILD-GUIDE.md` (sections 4, 5 "Truthfulness", 7), **`web/content/knowledge/_FACTS.md` first (the only source of business facts)**, `../Plan.md` sections "Knowledge files" (JSON format) and "Chatbot → Keeping relevance high" (golden set). The published products are listed in `web/seed/capture/products.json` (ignore the ones whose first image URL contains `bebitalia`, `_project_` or `outdoor_complement`: they are hidden).

## Create exactly three files
**1. `web/content/knowledge/faqs.json`**: a JSON array of about 80 objects: `{ "q": "…", "alt": ["…","…","…"], "a": "…", "tags": ["…"] }`.
- `alt` has 3–5 other ways people ask it, written the way they really type: short, lowercase, typos, "sofa set price", Singlish ("mila", "kiyada", "thiyenawada"), WhatsApp style. Sinhala/Tamil script is welcome only when you are sure of the words.
- `a` is 1–3 sentences, answer first, plain words. Facts only from `_FACTS.md`, with the hedge rules. Prices of unpriced pieces are "on request", confirmed at the showroom visit. Use `Rs.` for rupees.
- `tags` from: `store_info, delivery, warranty, materials, care, buying, colour, booking, about, custom, pricing, products`.
- Cover: where the showroom is, how to visit and book, hours (unknown: the team confirms), contact and WhatsApp, delivery and lead time (published claims, hedged), warranty (published claim, hedged), custom sizes/colours/fabrics, price and discounts and instalments (on request, hedged, past promos are not standing offers), what the cart/booking does and that no payment is online, materials (velvet, leather, corduroy, fabric), care and humidity, choosing L-shape/U-shape/3+2+1, small rooms, kids and pets, colour matching, products and categories, the velvet mattress cover sizes and price, whether recliners exist (the old site listed a recliner collection; say the team can advise), returns (unknown: team confirms), repairs and after-sales (published), about the company.
- Every question distinct; no duplicates; answers consistent with each other and with `_FACTS.md`.

**2. `web/content/knowledge/synonyms.json`**: one JSON object `{ "term": ["variant", …] }`. About 60 terms: English furniture words (sofa: couch, settee, sofa set; l-shape: corner sofa, sectional, l shaped, chaise sofa; recliner: reclining sofa; ottoman: pouf, footstool; coffee table: centre table, tea table, teapoy), materials (velvet, leather, corduroy, fabric, suede), colours, sizes (3 seater, 2 seater, single), money words (lakh, lakhs, k), Singlish ("sofa set mila", "kiyada", "thiyenawada", "delivery ekak"), and common Sinhala/Tamil furniture words you are certain of (සෝෆා, සෝපා, ඇඳ, පුටුව, මේසය, சோபா, கட்டில், நாற்காலி, மேசை) mapped to the English term. Keys are the canonical English term.

**3. `web/content/golden/golden.json`**: a JSON array of 60 objects, the chatbot's golden test set: `{ "id": "g01", "question": "…", "expect": { "intent": "find_products|product_question|cart_pairing|store_info|booking|small_talk|off_topic", "facts": ["substring that must appear in the answer or retrieved text"], "products": ["slug-or-name-fragment"], "handoff": false } }`. `intent` is required; use `facts` for store/delivery/booking/care questions (substrings must be wording that actually appears in your FAQ answers), `products` for product finds (use slugs from the published products, e.g. a green L-shape request expects `luxury-emerald-green-l-shape-sofa-modern-furniture-colombo`), `handoff: true` for questions the bot should not answer (returns policy, exact charges, hours). Mix: 15 store/booking/delivery, 15 product finds (colour, shape, material, budget in lakhs, seats), 8 product questions on a named piece, 6 cart pairing, 6 care/buying advice, 4 small talk, 3 off topic, 3 handoff. Write them as customers type them: short, lowercase, Singlish, typos.

## Rules
- Nothing invented. Unknown means unknown: hedge or hand off.
- Valid JSON only (double quotes, no comments, no trailing commas, UTF-8).
- Create only these three files. Do not modify any existing file. Reply in at most 4 lines.
