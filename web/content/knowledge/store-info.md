The showroom is in Dehiwala, Colombo. Visits are by appointment, so the team confirms a time with you.

## Where is the showroom?

The showroom is at 67 Pallidora Road, Dehiwala, Colombo. It is the place to see sofas, beds, chairs, tables, ottomans and cushions in person. The team can confirm the address again when you book your visit. Search for the address in Google Maps to see where it sits in Dehiwala. Come with your room measurements and any fabric ideas you have.

## How do I get directions to the showroom?

Open Google Maps and search for "Sofa Factory Outlet, 67 Pallidora Road, Dehiwala". The map pin shows the showroom on Pallidora Road. Message the team on WhatsApp if you would like directions while you travel. The team can also confirm the route on the day of your visit. Allow extra time if you are coming across Colombo at a busy hour.

## How do I contact the team on WhatsApp?

Message the team on WhatsApp at +94 76 671 1711. Use it for directions, a question before your visit, or a change to a booking. WhatsApp is the usual way to reach the team. Keep your booking reference handy when you message about a visit. The team will reply as soon as it can.

## Do I need to book a visit?

Yes, visits are booked on this website. Choose the pieces you would like to see, pick a day and a time slot, and send the booking. The team then confirms your visit by WhatsApp or phone. Booking ahead means the team knows when to expect you. It also gives you a clear time to plan your day around.

## What happens at a showroom visit?

At the visit you see the pieces in person and feel the fabrics. You can talk through sizes, colours and custom options with the team. The team confirms prices, delivery and warranty terms during your visit. No payment is taken online, so the visit is the place to settle the details. Bring photos of your room if you have them.

## When can I visit the showroom?

Visits are by appointment, and the team confirms a time that works for you. There are no fixed public opening hours listed in our current information. Ask the team on WhatsApp when you book if you need to know more about the timing. The team will answer with the current arrangement and confirm the time in writing.
