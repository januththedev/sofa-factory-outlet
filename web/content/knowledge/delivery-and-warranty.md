Our current information comes from what we published on our earlier website. The team confirms the exact terms for your order at the showroom visit.

## How long does delivery take?

Our current information is that delivery across Sri Lanka takes 10 to 15 working days. The team confirms the exact timing for your address at the visit. Treat the range as a guide rather than a promise. Ask the team to put the expected timing in writing when you order. Ask about the timing for a custom piece too.

## Is delivery free?

Our current information, as stated on our earlier website, is free islandwide delivery. Charges and exact terms are confirmed at your visit. Ask the team to confirm delivery to your area before you order. This is the safest way to know what applies to your address. If your building or street has access limits, mention them when you book.

## What does the warranty cover?

Our current information is a 10-year structural warranty. The terms are confirmed at your visit. Ask the team which parts of the sofa the structural cover includes. Ask also how a claim is handled and what you need to keep. The team will explain the details before you order. Get the answer in writing if the warranty matters to your decision.

## What should I do about repairs or adjustments?

Contact the team on WhatsApp at +94 76 671 1711. After-sales support covers repairs, adjustments and inquiries, as stated on our website. Explain what has happened and send a photo if you can. The team will tell you the next step and whether a visit is needed. Keep your order details and booking reference ready when you message.

## Can I return or exchange a sofa?

Returns and refunds are not stated in our current information. The team confirms the return and exchange policy at your visit. Ask before you order so you know where you stand. Ask about custom pieces in particular, because made-to-order items often have different rules. The team will explain what applies to your order.

## Do you deliver outside Colombo?

Yes, delivery covers Sri Lanka, and our current information is 10 to 15 working days. The team confirms the timing and any charges for your district at the visit. Ask the team about access to your building or street when you book. That helps the delivery team plan the last part of the journey. Tell the team about any gate, lift or stair that affects the drop-off.
