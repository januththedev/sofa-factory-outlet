Sofa Factory Outlet is a Sri Lankan furniture maker with its own Colombo workshop.

## Who are we?

Sofa Factory Outlet is one of Sri Lanka's trusted furniture manufacturers. We are known for premium quality sofas, beds, chairs and custom-made furniture at factory-direct prices. Our team is made up of furniture makers, designers and industry professionals. We are passionate about creating furniture that matches your lifestyle. Whether you are furnishing a new home, upgrading a living room or looking for custom-made sofas, we aim to offer premium quality at the best possible price.

## What is our mission?

Our mission is to deliver top quality furniture that blends comfort, style and affordability, all with fast and reliable service. We also put it this way: to provide Sri Lankans with high quality, handcrafted furniture made with comfort, durability and modern design in mind, at truly factory-direct prices.

## What is our vision?

Our vision is to become Sri Lanka's most trusted and affordable furniture brand. We want to offer a wide range of premium sofas, bedroom sets and custom-made solutions that enhance every home. We also want every customer to feel they received good value and good service, from the first visit to the delivery.

## Why choose Sofa Factory Outlet?

We give six reasons. We use durable fabrics, strong wooden frames and high-density cushioning. Every sofa is crafted by experienced technicians who focus on quality, comfort and finishing. You can choose your fabric, size, colour and layout. We offer islandwide free delivery, as stated on our website. We sell at factory-direct prices, with no middleman and no showroom markup. We also offer after-sales support for repairs, adjustments and inquiries.

## Where are your pieces made?

Every piece is built by hand at our own Colombo workshop. We use high-grade materials, skilled workmanship and modern production techniques. Being factory-direct means there is no middleman and no showroom markup. You can see the pieces in person at our showroom in Dehiwala. The team can tell you more about how a piece is made at your visit.

## What kind of furniture do you make?

We make sofas, beds and bedding, chairs, tables, ottomans, and pillows and cushions. Our sofa shapes include L-shape, U-shape modular, 3+2+1 sets, sets with wing chairs and Chesterfield-style pieces. We also make custom furniture to suit your space. Whether you are decorating an apartment, upgrading a living room or furnishing a new house, the team can help you choose.
