A visit is the easiest way to choose a sofa. These notes explain what happens before and during your appointment.

## How does a showroom visit work?

Choose the pieces you would like to see on the website. Pick a day and a time slot that suit you, then send the booking. The team confirms your visit by WhatsApp or phone. At the showroom you can see the pieces, feel the fabrics and ask questions. The team confirms prices and terms with you there. No payment is taken online.

## What is my booking reference?

Your booking gets a reference such as SFO-B-0042. Keep it handy when you message the team. The reference links your messages to your visit. Quote it when you change a booking or ask a question. The team can find your appointment faster with it. It also appears on your booking confirmation page.

## What is the calendar file for?

The calendar file adds your visit to your phone or computer calendar. Open the file and save the event. It then sits alongside your other appointments. The file is a reminder, not a confirmation of the time. The team still confirms the visit by WhatsApp or phone.

## How do I change or cancel my booking?

Message the team on WhatsApp at +94 76 671 1711. Give your booking reference and the change you need. Ask for a new day or time slot if the first one no longer suits. The team will confirm the new arrangement. Please message as early as you can so the slot can be offered to someone else.

## What should I bring to my visit?

Bring room measurements and a simple sketch of the space. Photos of the room help the team picture the light and layout. Bring fabric or colour ideas, and any swatches you already have. A photo of your doorway or lift is useful for delivery planning. If you have a floor plan, bring that too. The more the team knows, the easier the choice.

## Do I pay online?

No, no payment is taken online. The website is for choosing pieces and booking a visit. Prices are confirmed at the showroom visit. The team explains payment at that point. Ask about instalments or deposits at your visit if you need them. Get the terms in writing before you commit.

## How will the team confirm my visit?

The team confirms your visit by WhatsApp or phone. Watch for a message after you send the booking. Reply to confirm the time if asked. If you have not heard back, message the team on WhatsApp at +94 76 671 1711. Keep your booking reference ready so the team can find your appointment quickly.
