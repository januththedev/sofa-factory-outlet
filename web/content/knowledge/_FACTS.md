# Facts sheet: the only business facts writers may use

Everything below comes from the client's old website (captured 27 Oct 2026). **Nothing else about the business may be stated.** If a fact is not on this sheet it is unknown: do not guess, do not "round", do not borrow from other furniture shops. Write around unknowns with wording that stays true ("confirmed at your visit", "the team will confirm").

This file starts with an underscore, so the knowledge-base loader ignores it.

## Confirmed by being on the live site (still to be re-confirmed by the client)

- Name: **Sofa Factory Outlet**. Describes itself as "an artisanal sofa manufacturer creating unique pieces" and "one of Sri Lanka's trusted furniture manufacturers".
- Sofas, beds and bedding, chairs, tables, ottomans and pillows/cushions are the categories. Sofa shapes on the site: L-shape (corner/chaise), U-shape modular, 3+2+1 sofa sets, sets with wing chairs, Chesterfield-style. The old homepage also listed "Recliner Sofas" as a collection (no recliner is currently published). Materials named in product copy: velvet, leather, corduroy, fabric.
- Made in the client's **own Colombo workshop** ("Built at our Colombo workshop"); sold **factory-direct**, "no middleman, no showroom markup".
- **Custom orders**: customers can choose fabric, size, colour and layout; the old homepage says "we have 1000+ colors and designs".
- The About page's six reasons: premium materials (durable fabrics, strong wooden frames, high-density cushioning), skilled craftsmanship, custom designs, islandwide free delivery, factory-direct prices, after-sales support (repairs, adjustments and inquiries).
- Product copy repeats: a **10-year structural warranty**, and delivery **across Sri Lanka within 10 to 15 working days**. The About page says "Islandwide Free Delivery".
- The old homepage promos said: "up to 23% off" on a budget 3+2+1 set, "up to 15% off" on a luxury sofa set, and "12 months installment payment also available (T&C apply)". Treat these as past promotions, not standing offers.
- One priced product: a velvet fitted sheet/mattress cover in sizes single, queen, king and super king, Rs. 12,490 (usual price Rs. 15,000). All other products show "price on request".
- Showroom address (from the old site's map): **67 Pallidora Road, Dehiwala, Colombo**. WhatsApp: **+94 76 671 1711**. Social: Instagram `@sofa_factory_outlet`, a Facebook page called "Sofa Factory Outlet".
- The new site has no online payment. Customers choose pieces, **book a showroom visit** (date and time slot) and the team follows up. A booking gets a reference like `SFO-B-0042`, a calendar file and a WhatsApp button. Prices and terms are confirmed at the visit.

## Unknown: never state these

Opening hours and days; visit slot length; delivery charges or lead times by district; returns, refunds, exchanges; payment methods and instalment terms; deposit or advance rules; VAT status; bank details; a landline, email address or any other contact; staff names; years in business; number of customers served; awards; fabric brands or foam density figures; frame wood species; exact dimensions of any piece; stock levels; whether recliners are made; what the warranty covers beyond "structural".

## Wording rules

- Quote the client's published claims only with a hedge: "Our current information is a 10-year structural warranty; the terms are confirmed at your visit."
- Never promise a price, discount, delivery date or warranty outcome.
- Say "price on request" for unpriced pieces; the team confirms prices at the showroom visit.
- General furniture knowledge (how velvet wears, how to measure a room) is welcome as long as it is true in general and not presented as a Sofa Factory Outlet specification.
- British/Sri Lankan spelling (colour, metre, centre). Rupees as `Rs. 12,490`.
