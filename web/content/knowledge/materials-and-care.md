The notes below are general furniture knowledge. They are not a specification of any particular Sofa Factory Outlet piece; the team confirms the material of each piece at your visit.

## How does velvet feel and wear?

Velvet has a soft, dense pile that feels warm and smooth. It can show marks and shading where the pile is brushed one way or the other. Regular vacuuming with a soft brush helps keep the pile lifted. Blot spills straight away with a clean cloth instead of rubbing. Velvet is not the easiest fabric for a busy house, but it wears well when looked after.

## How do I care for leather?

Leather feels cool at first and softens with use. It develops a patina over time, which many people like. Wipe it with a damp cloth and dry it straight away. Avoid harsh cleaners and sharp objects. Keep leather out of strong direct sun, because it can dry and crack. A leather conditioner made for upholstery can help, but test it on a hidden patch first.

## What is corduroy like to live with?

Corduroy has soft raised ridges that feel warm and slightly velvety. It is generally hard-wearing, but the ridges can flatten with heavy use. Brush the pile gently to lift it again. Vacuum on a low setting with a soft attachment. Wipe spills quickly and avoid soaking the fabric. Corduroy suits a cosy room, though it can look more casual than a smooth weave.

## How do I clean general fabric?

Vacuum fabric regularly to lift dust and crumbs. Blot a spill with a clean cloth and work from the outside in. Use a mild soap solution on a small hidden patch first. Let the patch dry before you treat the whole area. For a deep clean, ask a professional upholstery cleaner. Harsh chemicals can set a stain or damage the weave, so go gently.

## How do I look after a sofa in Sri Lanka's humidity?

Keep air moving around the sofa, and pull it slightly away from damp walls. Leave a gap so air can reach the back and sides. Avoid pushing the sofa tight into a corner where moisture collects. Keep it out of strong direct sun, which can fade fabric and dry leather. Wipe away any condensation you notice. If mildew appears, clean it gently and let the piece dry fully in the air. A fan helps on still, damp days.

## What about kids and pets?

A tight weave usually copes better with claws and spills than a loose one. Darker colours and patterns hide marks more easily than pale plain fabrics. Keep pet claws trimmed and provide a throw where pets like to settle. Clean spills quickly, before they sink in. Removable covers are easier to wash, so ask the team whether a piece has them. A washable throw is a simple extra layer of protection.
